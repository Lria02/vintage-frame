03a / Our purpose

MISSION


To redefine the way memories are captured by bringing timeless vintage charm to modern celebrations across Australia.

VISION


At The Vintage Frame Studio, we are passionate about creating meaningful event experiences through beautifully curated photo booths and video guestbooks. By blending timeless vintage-inspired design with modern innovation, we capture genuine emotions, heartfelt messages, and joyful moments. Transforming every celebration into lasting memories that can be treasured for years to come.
